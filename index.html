Every guess leaves clues

After you guess, each tile changes color. Here is one guess and what it tells you.

3 8 1 6
  • Green: right digit, right spotThe code starts with 3.
  • Yellow: right digit, wrong spotThere's a 1 in the code, just not third.
  • Grey: not in the codeNo 8 and no 6 anywhere.

Harder puzzles can use the same digit twice, so a digit that lit up once might still appear again. Colorblind mode swaps green and yellow for blue and orange.

Questions

Is Digit Hunt free?

Yes. Every mode and level is free to play, with ads. Digit Hunt Pro is an optional one-time purchase, and Digit packs are optional top-ups for your Digits.

Does everyone get the same Daily Hunt?

Yes. Every player gets the same code on the same day, numbered Daily Hunt #1, #2 and so on. When you share your result, friends see your colored tiles, not the answer.

Can a digit appear more than once?

In harder puzzles, yes. The Daily Hunt, several campaign worlds and Infinite from Normal up can use the same digit twice.

What happens if I miss a day?

If you have a Streak Shield, it covers the missed day and your streak continues. You earn one for every 7-day run and can hold two. You can also get one in the Shop for Digits.

Do I need an internet connection?

For the free game, yes: ads keep Digit Hunt free, so it needs a connection to start a puzzle. If you lose signal mid-puzzle, it pauses until you're back online.

Digit Hunt Pro plays every mode offline.

Is there a colorblind mode?

Yes. Turn on Color Blind Mode in Settings to use blue and orange instead of green and yellow on the tiles. You can try it on the practice puzzle at the top of this page.

Does Pro remove every ad?

Pro removes the ads between puzzles and the ad on the Home screen. Rewarded ads stay available, and only play when you choose to watch one for Digits, a hint or an extra guess.

Does Pro unlock every theme and skin?

No. Pro comes with its own look, the Prism theme and Facet skin, which can't be bought with Digits, plus a gold app icon and a bonus of Digits. The other themes and skins are in the Shop for everyone, Pro or not.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the Google account you bought it with, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.

What happens to my Digits if I uninstall?

Your game, Digits included, is saved on your phone, so uninstalling or clearing the app's storage deletes it. That includes Digits from Digit packs. Pro is the exception: Google Play keeps it with your account.

Which languages and Android versions are supported?

Digit Hunt is available in English, हिन्दी, 日本語, 한국어 and Português (Brasil), and runs on Android 7.0 or later.
