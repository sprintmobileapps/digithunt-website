Every guess gives you clues

After you guess, each tile changes color. Here is one guess and what it tells you.

3 8 1 6
  • Green: right digit, right spotThe code starts with 3.
  • Yellow: right digit, wrong spotThere's a 1 in the code, just not third.
  • Grey: not in the codeNo 8 and no 6 anywhere.

Harder puzzles can use the same digit twice, so a digit that lit up once might still appear again.

Questions

Something else? Email sprintapps.dev@gmail.com.

Is Digit Hunt free?

Yes. Every mode and level is free to play, with ads. Digit Hunt Pro is an optional one-time purchase that removes the ads between puzzles.

Does everyone get the same Daily Challenge?

Yes. Every player gets the same code on the same day, numbered as Daily Hunt #1, #2 and so on. When you share your result, friends see your colored tiles, not the answer.

Can a digit appear more than once?

In harder puzzles, yes. The Daily Challenge, several campaign worlds and Infinite from Normal up can use the same digit twice.

What happens if I miss a day?

If you have a Streak Shield, it covers the missed day and your streak continues. You earn a shield for every 7-day run.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Puzzles are made on your phone, so you can play offline. You need a connection to watch an ad for Digits or to buy Pro.

Is there a colorblind mode?

Yes. Turn on Color Blind Mode in Settings to use blue and orange instead of green and yellow on the tiles.

Does Pro remove every ad?

Pro removes the ads that play between puzzles. Rewarded ads stay available, and only play when you choose to watch one for Digits or a hint.

I got a new phone. How do I get Pro back?

Sign in with the Google account you bought it with, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase.

Which languages and Android versions are supported?

Digit Hunt is available in English, हिन्दी, 日本語, 한국어 and Português (Brasil), and runs on Android 7.0 or later.
