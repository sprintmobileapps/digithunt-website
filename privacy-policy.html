Privacy Policy

Digit Hunt for Android. Last updated October 2026.

In short

  • There is no account. Digit Hunt never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress, streak and settings are saved on your device.
  • Google services handle ads (AdMob), anonymous analytics and crash reports (Firebase) and the Pro purchase (Google Play).

1. What Digit Hunt is

Digit Hunt is a number-guessing puzzle game published by Sprint Mobile Apps. There is no account or login, and the game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game lives on your device:

  • Progress: puzzles played and won, your guesses for each puzzle, streak, Streak Shields, XP and level, Digits balance, campaign progress and achievements
  • Preferences: theme, colorblind mode, sound, haptics, timer, reminder settings and the cosmetics you own or have equipped
  • Purchase status: whether this device has Digit Hunt Pro (a yes/no flag, not tied to any identity)

This data stays on your device. Android may include it in your device's own backup (Google One or Android Backup) if you have that turned on.

3. Analytics and crash reporting

Digit Hunt uses two Google Firebase services to improve the game.

Firebase Analytics records how the game is played: for example, that a puzzle was started, won or abandoned (with the mode, number of guesses, hints used and time taken), that a hint or rewarded ad was used, that Digits were earned or spent, that a streak continued, that a setting was changed, or that a result was shared (and to which app). It also keeps rough, bucketed traits such as your player level or streak length. These events are tied to an anonymous per-install identifier (the Firebase App Instance ID), not to your name or any account. Purchases of Digit Hunt Pro are reported by Google Play to Firebase as purchase events.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and error logs (stack traces, device model, Android version and app version) so we can find and fix bugs.

Both are provided by Google and governed by Firebase's privacy terms.

4. Advertising

Digit Hunt shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (watched to earn Digits, a hint or an extra guess) and occasional interstitial ads between puzzles. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID: a resettable device identifier used for ad personalisation and frequency capping
  • IP address: used to estimate your region
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them

This data is collected by Google, not by Sprint Mobile Apps.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Digit Hunt asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Digit Hunt Pro removes interstitial ads. Rewarded ads stay available, and only play when you choose to watch one.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchases

Digit Hunt Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase went through.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Digit Hunt can remind you about the Daily Challenge and warn you when your streak is at risk. Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn each one off in Settings → Notifications or in Android's settings.

7. Ratings

Digit Hunt may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google Play handles the review; the app never sees your rating, your review or your account.

8. Children

Digit Hunt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us at the address below.

11. Data retention and deletion

To delete everything Digit Hunt stores on your device, go to Settings → Apps → Digit Hunt → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Analytics data is kept by Firebase for a limited period and then deleted automatically. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, email us. Resetting your advertising ID and reinstalling also starts a fresh, unlinked identity.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle data, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, mention it in the Play Store release notes.
