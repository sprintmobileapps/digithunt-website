Privacy Policy

Digit Hunt for Android. Last updated November 2026.

In short

  • Digit Hunt has no account of its own. It never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress, streak and settings are saved on your device.
  • Google services handle ads (AdMob), anonymous analytics and crash reports (Firebase), and purchases of Pro and Digit packs (Google Play).
  • Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. It saves a copy of your achievements to your Play Games profile.

1. What Digit Hunt is

Digit Hunt is a number-guessing puzzle game published by Sprint Mobile Apps. It has no account of its own and no sign-up, and the game never asks for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. You can choose to sign in to Google Play Games to keep your achievements there (section 9).

2. Data stored on your device

Your game lives on your device:

  • Progress: puzzles played and won, your guesses for each puzzle, streak, Streak Shields, XP and level, Digits balance, campaign progress and achievements
  • Preferences: theme, colorblind mode, sound, haptics, timer, reminder settings and the cosmetics you own or have equipped
  • Purchase status: whether this device has Digit Hunt Pro and the Starter Pack, and the Google Play purchase tokens already paid into your Digits, so no purchase is paid out twice. None of this is tied to your name or an account.

This data stays on your device. Android may include it in your device's own backup (Google One or Android Backup) if you have that turned on.

3. Analytics and crash reporting

Digit Hunt uses two Google Firebase services to improve the game.

Firebase Analytics records how the game is played: for example, that a puzzle was started, won or abandoned (with the mode, number of guesses, hints used and time taken), that a hint or rewarded ad was used, that Digits were earned or spent, that a streak continued, that a setting was changed, that a result was shared (and to which app), or whether a Google Play Games sign-in you started worked. It also keeps rough, bucketed traits such as your player level or streak length, and whether you're signed in to Google Play Games. These events are tied to an anonymous per-install identifier (the Firebase App Instance ID), not to your name or any account. Purchases of Digit Hunt Pro and of Digit packs are reported by Google Play to Firebase as purchase events.

Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports and error logs (stack traces, device model, Android version and app version) so we can find and fix bugs.

Both are provided by Google and governed by Firebase's privacy terms.

4. Advertising

Digit Hunt shows ads served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (watched to earn Digits, double a puzzle's rewards, get a hint or an extra guess, or see a puzzle's full hunt analysis), occasional interstitial ads between puzzles and, for players who have played for a while, an ad card on the Home screen. AdMob may collect:

  • Advertising ID: a resettable device identifier used for ad personalisation and frequency capping
  • IP address: used to estimate your region
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown and whether you interacted with them

This data is collected by Google, not by Sprint Mobile Apps.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Digit Hunt asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.

Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Digit Hunt Pro removes interstitial ads and the Home screen ad card. Rewarded ads stay available, and only play when you choose to watch one.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. In-app purchases

Digit Hunt Pro is a one-time purchase, and Digit packs are optional purchases of in-game Digits. Both are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the purchase went through. Digits bought in a pack are kept on your device with the rest of your progress and can only be used in Digit Hunt.

6. Connection and date check

So the Daily Hunt and your streak follow the real date whatever your phone's clock says, Digit Hunt asks Google's servers for the current date and time when it starts. This is a plain HTTPS request to www.google.com that carries no game data and no identifiers, though like any request it shows Google your IP address.

The app also asks Android whether you're online, because the free version needs a connection to play. Your connection status isn't stored or sent anywhere.

7. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Digit Hunt can remind you about the Daily Challenge and warn you when your streak is at risk. Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can turn each one off in Settings → Notifications or in Android's settings.

8. Ratings

Digit Hunt may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google Play handles the review; the app never sees your rating, your review or your account.

9. Google Play Games

Digit Hunt can save your achievements to your Google Play Games profile. This is optional: the game, your progress and your achievements in the app work the same without it.

  • Signing in: if you already have a Play Games profile with automatic sign-in turned on, Google signs you in when the app starts. Otherwise nothing happens until you tap Sign in on the Achievements screen, and Google's own sign-in screen takes it from there. Digit Hunt never sees your password.
  • What the app sends: which achievements you have unlocked and your progress towards the others. The rest of your game stays on your device.
  • What the app reads: only whether you're signed in. Digit Hunt does not ask Play Games for your name, email address, gamer ID, friends or profile picture.

Google keeps your Play Games profile and achievements with your Google account, and other players may see them depending on your Play Games profile settings. You can turn off automatic sign-in and change who sees your profile in the Play Store app (profile icon → Settings → General → Play Games profile). Read Google's guide to Play Games profile settings.

10. Children

Digit Hunt is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, contact us at the address below.

13. Data retention and deletion

To delete everything Digit Hunt stores on your device, go to Settings → Apps → Digit Hunt → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This also gives the app a new analytics ID, so earlier analytics and crash data is no longer linked to your device. Firebase deletes Analytics event data within 14 months and Crashlytics crash reports after 90 days. Resetting your advertising ID cuts the link to past ad data held by Google. Achievements saved to Google Play Games stay on your Play Games profile until you delete Digit Hunt's game data there; clearing the app's storage doesn't remove them.

Step-by-step instructions, including what is kept, are on the Delete your data page.

14. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle data, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, mention it in the Play Store release notes.
