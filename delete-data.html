Delete your data

Digit Hunt for Android, by Sprint Mobile Apps. Last updated November 2026.

Before you start

Digit Hunt has no account of its own, so there is nothing to close. Your game is saved on your phone, and you can delete it yourself in a minute. If you signed in to Google Play Games, step 4 removes the achievements saved there. The steps below cover every kind of data the app collects, what gets deleted and what is kept.

1. Delete your game data on your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps → Digit Hunt → Storage.
  3. Tap Clear storage. You can also uninstall Digit Hunt instead.

Deleted straight away: your progress, streak, Streak Shields, XP and level, Digits (including any from Digit packs), guesses, stats, achievements, settings and the cosmetics you own.

Kept: your Digit Hunt Pro purchase. Google Play keeps it with your Google account, so you can restore it later from the Pro screen. If Android backup is turned on, a copy of your game may stay in your Google One backup until that backup is replaced or you delete it in Google One.

2. Analytics and crash reports

Digit Hunt sends anonymous gameplay events to Firebase Analytics and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. They are tied to a random ID for your install, not to your name or an account.

When you clear storage or reinstall (step 1), the app gets a new ID, so the analytics and crash data from before is no longer linked to your phone. Firebase then deletes that older data automatically:

  • Firebase Analytics events: deleted within 14 months
  • Crashlytics crash reports: deleted after 90 days

3. Advertising data

Ads in Digit Hunt come from Google AdMob, which collects your advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions. Google holds this data, not Sprint Mobile Apps.

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Go to Google → Ads.
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID on older Android versions).

This cuts the link between your phone and past ad data. Google keeps ad data as described in its data retention policy. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also change your ad consent in Digit Hunt under Settings → Privacy options.

4. Google Play Games achievements

If you signed in to Google Play Games, a copy of your Digit Hunt achievements is kept on your Play Games profile by Google. Clearing the app's storage doesn't remove it. To delete it:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app and tap your profile icon.
  2. Go to Settings → General → Play Games profile → Privacy & settings.
  3. Tap Delete profile & game data.
  4. Under Delete individual game data, find Digit Hunt and tap Delete.

On a computer, go to play.google.com/games/profile and open Your data. Menu names can differ between versions; Google's help page has the current steps. Only Digit Hunt's data is removed. If the app still has your achievements and you're signed in, it sends them to Play Games again the next time it opens, so clear its storage (step 1) too if you want them gone everywhere.
